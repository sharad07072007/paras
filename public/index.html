Areas of practice

Assessment follows classical Ayurvedic diagnosis (Prakriti constitution and Nadi Pariksha) before any treatment plan is customized.

01

Digestive Health (Gastrointestinal)

Acidity, IBS, chronic constipation, bloating, and metabolic sluggishness managed through gut agni restoration and targeted herbal formulations.

02

Joint & Spine Care (Sandhivata)

Arthritis, back pain, sciatica, and cervical stiffness addressed with specialized Panchakarma therapies (Janu/Kati Basti) and internal medicine.

03

Skin Conditions (Kushtha Chikitsa)

Eczema, psoriasis, allergic dermatitis, and acne treated by blood purification (Raktamokshana / detox) rather than surface suppression.

04

Women's Health (Stree Roga)

Menstrual irregularities, PCOS/PCOD, menopausal wellness, and hormonal balance supported through classical dietary and botanical protocols.

05

Neurological & Stress Disorders (Vata-Manas Vikara)

Migraine, vertigo, stress, and sleep disturbances managed through tailored Ayurvedic protocols to restore nervous system balance.

06

Chronic Disease Support

Supportive complementary care for diabetes, hypertension, and fatty liver disease alongside your ongoing modern medical prescriptions.

07

Respiratory & Allergic Disorders (Pranavaha Srotas)

Personalised Ayurvedic care for allergic rhinitis, sinusitis, asthma, and recurrent respiratory concerns, with a focus on long-term respiratory wellness.
